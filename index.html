About Smart Canteen

Smart Canteen lets students pre-order food, drinks and snacks and get them delivered to their classroom, so there is no queue to stand in. Stock updates as items are ordered.

How to order

  1. Log in with your Enrollment ID (create an account the first time).
  2. Tap the heart on items you like. They show up under Favourites.
  3. Press Add to Cart. Items stay in your cart until you remove them or place the order.
  4. Open Cart, check quantities, then enter your class, floor, room, arrival time and payment method.
  5. Follow your order under Track Order until it is delivered.

Rules

  • Use only your own Enrollment ID. One account per student, and never share your password.
  • You can order only what is in stock.
  • Enter the correct class, floor and room. Orders go to the details you give.
  • Be in your classroom at the arrival time you choose.
  • Fake or prank orders may get your account blocked by canteen staff.
  • Log out when using a shared computer.

Your data

This version keeps accounts, carts, favourites and orders in this browser only. Passwords are hashed, but this is a project demo: a real deployment needs a server and database.
